This scoping review followed JBI methodology and PRISMA-ScR. … Scopus, Web of Science Core Collection, and ERIC were searched with a cut-off of 21 September 2026 without publication-year limits. … The synthesis included 119 reports, comprising 72 core empirical reports on primary English education, 31 adjacent empirical reports from related settings and 16 contextual sources. … The synthesis organised the evidence into five recurring pedagogical functions. … These covered oral interaction and speaking rehearsal, pronunciation and oral-reading feedback, and writing, vocabulary, and feedback scaffolding. … They also included reading and multimodal meaning-making and teacher-facing assessment and materials design.
