This developmental research study (Type I) reports the design and preliminary validation of an AI-enhanced game-based learning environment (AI-GBLE) for upper-secondary students. … Following expert appraisal of content, instructional design, and media-technology quality by nine specialists, three per domain, 30 Grade 11 students in Thailand used the environment across eight 60-minute sessions over six weeks in a one-group pretest–posttest validation. … Because the single-group design precludes causal attribution, these findings are interpreted as preliminary evidence—of feasibility, acceptability, and learning-related signals from a developed prototype—rather than as intervention effectiveness.
