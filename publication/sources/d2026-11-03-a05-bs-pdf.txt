This qualitative case study investigated how 12 Chinese Grade 7 students engaged with generative artificial intelligence (AI) over a six-week project on the collapse of the Qin Dynasty. … Drawing on an established AI literacy framework, the study adapted its dimensions to develop a framework tailored to historical inquiry, comprising four dimensions: AI Prompting Competence, AI Information Credibility, AI Bias Verification, and AI Ethics and Safety. … Three themes emerged: developing historical questions and extending inquiry through AI interaction, critically evaluating AI outputs, and strategic engagement with AI in historical inquiry.
