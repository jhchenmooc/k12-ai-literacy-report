Abstract excerpt (PDF p. 1 (p. 115)):
Drawing on sociocultural and cultural-historical activity theory, we qualitatively analyzed group interviews with 40 K–12 students in grades 6 and 9. The results show that students fluently used AI concepts as mediating tools.

Abstract excerpt (PDF p. 1 (p. 115)):
students relied on scientific explanations and used AI concepts to explain classifier behavior, social media mechanisms, user engagement, and filter bubbles. Methods excerpt (PDF p. 8 (p. 122), §4):
The three-year (2023-2025) process of co-designing cumulative AI learning paths involved researchers from computer science and education, teachers and school principals from 12 primary and secondary schools, as well as over 200 students (4th and 7th graders at the beginning of the project).

Participants and data collection excerpt (PDF p. 10 (p. 124), §4.2):
This study collected data from reflective interviews organized in spring 2025 right after the third year interventions. They involved students from 8 different schools and consisted of 14 group interviews (7 conducted in secondary schools, 7 in primary schools), with a total of 40 participants.
