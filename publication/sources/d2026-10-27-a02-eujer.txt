A systematic literature review following the PRISMA 2020 guidelines was conducted to study implementations of Generative AI and methods of AI literacy education in elementary education. … The review data includes 211 studies published from 2021 to January 2026 identified from SCOPUS, EBSCO, Web of Science, and Taylor & Francis. … The analysis identifies fundamental issues affecting the implementation of Generative AI in elementary education, including concerns about human interaction, varying approaches to ethical questions, and frequent concerns regarding the reliability of Generative AI outputs. … Established use cases of Generative AI are identified, especially with self-regulated learning and foreign language studies.
