In response to this research gap, the present study expands the Theory of Planned Behavior (TPB) by integrating AI literacy, facilitating conditions and AI anxiety, thereby developing a more integrative motivational framework for investigating teachers’ intentions to teach AI. … Using cross-sectional survey data from 218 secondary school information technology teachers, covariance-based structural equation modeling was employed to test the hypothesized relationships. … Machine learning classification analyses were additionally conducted to examine the predictive performance of the theoretically relevant variables.
