Owing to the scarcity of empirical data regarding how Primary Education students perceive Artificial Intelligence (AI), this research examined variations based on gender, educational cycle, attendance at the participating rural or urban schools, and usage habits. … The participant group included 391 students from the second and third cycles (Grades 3 to 6). … Data collection involved a 22-item instrument adapted from Chai et al. (2021), focusing on five distinct areas: self-efficacy, readiness, social benefit, literacy, and future intent. … The findings revealed overall positive attitudes, without notable gender-based disparities.
