This study examined changes in computational thinking (CT), AI concept mastery, learning motivation, and programming self-efficacy following a tangible AI intervention, and explored the teacher's implementation experience. … Guided by a framework integrating the AI Literacy Framework and the SEAME Model, a four-week intervention (4 × 60 minutes) was implemented with 36 fifth-grade students and one teacher in a public elementary school, using a single-group pretest–posttest design within a mixed-methods approach. … Quantitative data were analyzed using repeated-measures ANCOVA, with prior computing experience as a covariate; qualitative data from semi-structured teacher interviews (during and after the intervention) and observations of all four sessions were analyzed thematically. … Tangible AI may offer a promising bridge to early AI literacy; however, without a control group, changes cannot be causally attributed to the intervention, and these single-school findings are preliminary.
