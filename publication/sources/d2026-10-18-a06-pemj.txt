This study investigated the experiences of Filipino teachers at Olutanga National High School in using AI in their teaching, guided by the TPACK framework. … Using a qualitative case study design, five Filipino teachers with direct experience using AI were purposively selected. … Findings showed that teachers used AI tools such as ChatGPT, Grammarly, QuillBot, and Canva AI primarily for lesson preparation, writing support, and visual materials development. … Teachers identified continuous and context-specific training, clear ethical guidelines, and improved technical infrastructure as the support most needed for the responsible use of AI, support that remained largely inadequate at the time of the study.
