This study examined AI adoption configurations among lower-secondary teachers in European education systems that administered the TALIS 2024 survey. … Using a quantitative, cross-sectional secondary analysis design, the study analyzed data from 13,440 teachers across 31 systems who provided complete responses and held determinate attitudes to AI-attitude indicators. … Three AI-attitude profiles were retained as benefit-oriented, cautious, and skeptical. … Moreover, four AI-adoption configurations were identified as benefit-oriented broad-use, cautious non-use, skeptical non-use, and cautious selective-use.
