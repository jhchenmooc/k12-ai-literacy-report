Abstract excerpt (PDF p. 2 (p. 1)):
Using a two-year longitudinal qualitative design, seventy-four K-12 school teachers were interviewed, and data were analyzed via deductive thematic analysis. Findings revealed that the three SDT needs satisfaction fostered pedagogical innovation, efficiency, and professional community. Conversely, needs frustration undermined high-quality integration, which is caused by external control, skill gaps, isolation, and ethical disengagement. These results demonstrate that AI integration is fundamentally a motivational challenge, not merely a technical one.
