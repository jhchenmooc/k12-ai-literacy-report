This cross-national study examined AI leadership readiness in China and Kazakhstan, focusing on scenario understanding, instructional value, risk concern, digital leadership self-efficacy, organizational support, leadership support for adoption, implementation readiness, and competence gaps. … We analyzed a cross-national quantitative survey of 763 school leaders (China n = 389; Kazakhstan n = 374) using reliability analysis, measurement invariance testing, latent profile analysis, standardized recursive path modeling, and importance–competence gap analysis. … Positive instructional appraisals frequently coexisted with moderate school-level preparation. … Ethics and risk governance, teacher development, and content quality audit emerged as the leading priorities for professional learning.
