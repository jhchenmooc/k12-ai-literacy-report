This pilot study explores the integration of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) into primary STEM education in Sweden through a structured Teacher Professional Development (TPD) model. … Eleven teachers participated in a six-month TPD programme comprising workshops, collaborative discussions, and classroom implementations. … The empirical material consisted primarily of audio-recorded workshop discussions, which were transcribed and analysed, supplemented by a post-intervention questionnaire. … Teachers reported opportunities for timesaving, differentiated instruction, and enhanced creativity, alongside challenges related to mathematical inaccuracies, unrealistic time estimates, and the need for contextual adaptation.
