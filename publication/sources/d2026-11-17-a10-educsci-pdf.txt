This study reports the development and initial validation of a GenAI stress scale tailored to Chinese K-12 teachers (T-GenAISS). … Exploratory analysis on a randomly split half of 612 teachers (174 pre-service and 438 in-service) affiliated with an Alliance of Thinking Schools professional learning community (n = 306) suggested seven dimensions: Privacy Violation, Job Replacement, Professional Development, Against Ethics, Lack of Transparency, Technological Dependence, and Bias Behavior. … The study provides initial psychometric evidence for a seven-factor structure in this ATS-affiliated sample and identifies the substantive refinements that independent replication must address before the instrument is used for diagnostic or evaluative purposes.
