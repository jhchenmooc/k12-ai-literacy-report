This pioneering study used the Context-Mechanism-Outcomes (CMO) schema as an organising framework to explore how Australian ECEC educators use GenAI tools, including the Contexts in which they are used, the Mechanisms of use, and the associated Outcomes. … Using an online qualitative survey of 75 participants across Australia, guided by critical realism and abductive reasoning, this exploratory study shows that GenAI users outnumbered non-users in this sample, and four distinct, plausible explanatory use patterns emerged.
