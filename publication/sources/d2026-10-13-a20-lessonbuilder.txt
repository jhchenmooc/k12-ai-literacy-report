We conducted two complementary qualitative studies with eight secondary computing teachers: focus groups examining existing authoring practices and a think-aloud study using LessonBuilder, a structured AI-supported lesson authoring system, as a research probe. … We found that teachers' instructional intentions developed through creating, inspecting, and adapting materials, while teachers selectively delegated tasks to AI and retained responsibility for judging classroom readiness.
