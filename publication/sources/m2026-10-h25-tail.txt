Most instruments that claim to measure AI literacy inquire how competent people feel rather than test what they know, and almost none has been validated for both students and teachers.
We evaluated it among Czech secondary-school students tested in fall 2025 (n = 2,441) and six months later (n = 1,669; 897 linked across the two measurement waves), and among teachers (n = 564).
Teachers' self-rated AI self-efficacy was practically unrelated to their tested knowledge, and students who used and relied on AI more knew less about it.
