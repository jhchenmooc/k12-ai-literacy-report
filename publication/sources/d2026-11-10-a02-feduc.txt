The aim of this study is to examine educational administrators’ opinions about use of AI in educational administration process. … The study was conducted using a qualitative case study design. … Data were collected through a semi-structured interview form and analyzed using content analysis. … The findings revealed that educational administrators primarily define AI as digital tools that perform data analysis, imitate human intelligence, and facilitate routine tasks. … The most common areas of AI use in administration processes were identified as planning, communication and official correspondence, and data analysis.
