This study assessed chemistry teachers' research competence, examined their reported practices and perceptions concerning students' social-emotional well-being, and investigated changes following participation in an AI-supported professional development program. … A quantitatively driven mixed-methods design with an embedded qualitative component was employed. … Data were collected from 105 chemistry teachers using closed-ended questionnaire scales and two open-ended questions administered before and after a six-week professional development program.
