An interpretive qualitative design was adopted using an open-ended questionnaire completed by 183 teachers from 15 Spanish autonomous communities.
The findings indicate that insufficient training and limited digital competence constitute the main barriers to GAI integration, while ethical concerns focus primarily on the potential decline in students’ critical thinking, irresponsible use, data privacy, and the reliability of AI-generated information.
