This study develops and tests an integrated model that draws on selected constructs from the Technology Acceptance Model 3 (TAM3) and the Theory of Planned Behavior (TPB) and incorporates artificial intelligence (AI) literacy to explain early childhood education (ECE) educators’ behavioral intention to use AI. … Using partial least squares structural equation modeling to analyze survey data from 1,696 Chinese ECE educators (97.9% female), who were recruited primarily from the public sector, the integrated model accounted for 89.2% of the variance in behavioral intention. … Theoretically, the integrated model offers a coherent framework for understanding Chinese ECE educators’ intentions to adopt AI at work.
