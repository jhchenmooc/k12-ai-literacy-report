This mixed-methods study examines AI use and non-use, attitudes, and socialization contexts among 14- to 17-year-olds based on six focus groups (N = 24) and a representative survey (N = 610). … AI is used mainly in school and leisure contexts, while non-use stems from lack of access, mistrust, privacy concerns, and limited knowledge. … Families and schools mainly convey fears and rules, whereas peers and independent experimentation fill knowledge gaps.
