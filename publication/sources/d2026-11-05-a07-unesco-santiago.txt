The UNESCO Regional Office in Santiago commemorated the 25th anniversary of the Information for All Programme (IFAP) with two regional events held in Montevideo and Santiago. … The activities brought together specialists from Latin America and the Caribbean to reflect on the challenges posed by artificial intelligence (AI) for education, media and information literacy, and information integrity. … Implemented through the UNESCO Observatory on Artificial Intelligence in Education for Latin America and the Caribbean, the project seeks to strengthen critical AI literacy among teachers, students and education authorities by promoting the capacities needed to understand, evaluate and use these technologies ethically, critically and responsibly.
