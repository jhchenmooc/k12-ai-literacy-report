The current study aimed to examine the effectiveness of a training program designed for improving teachers’ knowledge and attitudes toward the use of applications (apps) enhanced with artificial intelligence (AI) to develop the language skills of Deaf and Hard-of-hearing (DHH) students. … The study sample consisted of 31 female elementary school teachers of DHH students in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. … The study measured changes in knowledge and attitudes before and after the intervention. … A quasi-experimental approach was applied that incorporated a scale and program developed by the author.
