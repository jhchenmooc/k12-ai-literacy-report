This study employs psychological network modeling to examine the interrelationships among science teachers’ perceptions of benefits, challenges, and attitudes toward integrating artificial intelligence (AI) tools in science curriculum development. … A total of 691 Saudi science teachers completed a validated questionnaire. … Centrality analyses indicated that curriculum content generation and innovative pedagogical tools were most central, while data processing challenges and algorithmic bias emerged as prominent structural barriers. … Teacher attitudes demonstrate ambivalence, characterized by resistance to automating administrative functions alongside guarded optimism for interactive and adaptive learning applications.
