This study aims to systematically evaluate the usability, acceptability and pedagogical relevance of an AIED-Unplugged learning analytics tool from teachers' perspectives, in order to guide the development of user-centered design strategies and support writing practices in resource-constrained educational settings without deepening digital inequities. … We employed a mixed-methods approach involving nine teachers, who downloaded an AIED-U application and followed a predefined usage scenario according to a think-aloud protocol. … Afterwards, participants filled out the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) and System Usability Scale (SUS) questionnaires, taking part in a moderated semi-structured interview guided by an interview script. … The quantitative findings indicated high levels of acceptance and usability, corroborating the qualitative findings that teachers perceived the solution as useful, easy to integrate and supportive of their practices. … The usability testing of the Escreva Mais was conducted with nine teachers from private and public high schools in Brazil.
