Abstract excerpt (Introduction sentence 1, Methods sentence 1, Discussion sentences 2 and 3, from the publisher article page; joined by " … "):
This study examined changes in students' self-reported creativity-related behaviors following an educational intervention combining artificial intelligence (AI) tools and the DRAE instructional model (Diagnosis, Recreation, Application, and Estimation). … A one-group preexperimental pretest-posttest questionnaire design was complemented by semi-structured interviews and direct-observation records. … However, retrospective record linkage, limitations in cross-instrument correspondence, self-report measurement, and the absence of a comparison group restrict causal interpretation. … The findings do not establish an independent causal effect of AI or validate the DRAE model.
