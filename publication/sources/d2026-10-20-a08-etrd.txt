To address this gap, this study develops AIgnite, an AI literacy toolkit for upper primary school students in Grades 4–6. … The toolkit combines AI knowledge cards with two stages supported by these cards: (i) the Board Game stage and (ii) the AI Storydrawing stage. … Developed using a child-centered approach, the toolkit underwent multiple rounds of participatory co-creation and iterative prototyping with 30 participants. … A user study with another 42 participants demonstrated the toolkit’s effectiveness in conveying AI concepts and applications and in fostering ethical reflection.
