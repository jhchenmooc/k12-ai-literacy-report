Adoption of artificial intelligence in K-12 schools has outpaced guidance for students, teachers, and administrators. … We propose an AI Security Literacy (AISL) framework of six dimensions spanning the school ecosystem, ground its technical dimensions in public AI-safety benchmarks and a reproducible multi-seed adversarial evaluation, and ground its attitudinal dimensions in nationally representative survey micro-data from U.S. teachers and adults. … NeuralFortify, a browser-based K-12 platform built on real AI models, is presented as an illustrative operationalization.
