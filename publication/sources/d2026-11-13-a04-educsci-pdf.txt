This protocol describes a two-arm cluster-randomised trial in schools that follow the Cambridge Lower Secondary Mathematics framework. … Grade 8 mathematics teachers will be randomised within schools to AIMAS-8, a system that combines a concept-level dynamic Bayesian learner model, prerequisite-aware item selection and expert-authored misconception feedback, or to the same items delivered in a fixed order with worked solutions. … Both arms receive twelve 30 min sessions on linear relationships. … No participant data have been collected.
