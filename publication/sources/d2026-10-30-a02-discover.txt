This study examines how the spontaneous, largely unmediated adoption of AI tools in secondary school learning practices shapes student motivation and engagement in Morocco, and what these outcomes imply for the pedagogical design of AI-integrated instruction. … Conducted within a post-positivist, mixed-methods paradigm, the study draws on a structured survey administered to 100 secondary school students (Years 10–12, science and humanities streams) during the 2024–2025 academic year. … Data were collected via Google Forms and analyzed through descriptive statistics, non-parametric inferential tests, and thematic content analysis of open-ended responses. … Results indicate that 97.0% of students report familiarity with AI, and 89.0% use ChatGPT as their primary learning tool.
