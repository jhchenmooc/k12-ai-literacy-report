Abstract excerpt (abstract sentences 4, 5, 6 and 8; joined by " … "):
In the present study, 7 middle school mathematics teachers used ChatGPT to create personalized versions of problems in their curriculum to correspond to their students’ interests. … The prompting moves teachers made and their students’ open-ended feedback on personalized problems were examined through thematic analysis. … Teachers’ efficiency when creating problems and the ratings of their 7th grade students who received personalized assignments were examined. … Teachers used prompting to adjust popular culture references and address issues with the depth, ethics, or realism of the problems generated, in some cases showing epistemic agency.
Method, Sample (body text, one sentence): Teachers taught 7th grade mathematics in 6 different US schools; 2 teachers identified as male and 5 as female.
