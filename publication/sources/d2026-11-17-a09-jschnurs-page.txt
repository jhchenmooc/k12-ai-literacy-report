School nurses need workplace-based training to use generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) responsibly in practice; this study developed and preliminarily evaluated a task-oriented GenAI continuing professional development program. … Using a multiphase multiple-methods preliminary evaluation guided by instructional design and self-directed learning theory, 42 school nurses from 22 counties and cities in Taiwan completed an 18-h blended program comprising prompt-scaffolded instruction, workplace assignments, an online booster session, and peer sharing. … Self-directed learning and critical thinking were assessed at four time points, assignment performance was rated using task-specific rubrics, and follow-up interviews explored workplace application.
