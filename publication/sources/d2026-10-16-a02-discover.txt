In this context, the aim of this study is to develop a valid and reliable scale to obtain accurate data on artificial intelligence literacy by examining the concept of artificial intelligence literacy. … The study sample consists of 870 students (424 boys, 346 girls) in grades 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 attending elementary and middle schools. … A pool of 50 items was created to measure students’ levels of artificial intelligence literacy. … Exploratory Factor Analysis (EFA) resulted in 22 items and 6 subscales. … The measurement model obtained from Confirmatory Factor Analysis (CFA) demonstrated good fit.
