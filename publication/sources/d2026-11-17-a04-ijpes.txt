This study reassessed the validity of the AI Dependence Scale among 316 secondary school students (51.3% male; age 16–19 years, M = 17.30) from three educational directorates. … Participants completed measures of AI dependence, reading comprehension beliefs, and AI-use stigma. … A unidimensional structure was confirmed, accounting for 54.7% of variance with loadings of 0.654–0.782 (EFA) and 0.704–0.835 (CFA).
