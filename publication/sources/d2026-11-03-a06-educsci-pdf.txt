This study presents the design and preliminary evaluation of a guided experiential workshop on AI-enabled physical systems for secondary-school students. … Students worked with programmable electronics, camera input, image datasets, machine-learning classification, and actuator responses. … A one-group pre-test/post-test design examined immediate changes in workshop-specific foundational knowledge and exploratory associations with student-profile indicators. … These findings support further refinement and evaluation; they do not establish causal effects, broader AI literacy gains, or independent design competence.
