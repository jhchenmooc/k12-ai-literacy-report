Abstract excerpt (abstract sentences 2, 3, 4 and the last sentence; joined by " … "):
This study examines the interrelationships among self-efficacy, engagement, attitudes, behavioral intentions, and perceived learning in secondary STEM education. … The study was based on real-world classroom teaching involving 710 students, who engaged with an AI learning companion that provided personalized guidance and support during STEM laboratory courses. … Using a single-group, post-test-only design and partial least squares structural equation modeling (PLS-SEM), the study examined concurrent associations among students' self-reported perceptions. … These findings describe concurrent associations and do not establish that changes in learning are attributable to the AI learning companion.
