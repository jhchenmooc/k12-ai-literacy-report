This study aimed to explore the perceptions and implementation challenges of science and mathematics teachers regarding the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in education. … A descriptive cross-sectional survey design was employed. … Data were collected through an online questionnaire administered to 218 Jordanian science and mathematics teachers working in public schools during the 2023/2024 academic year. … Results revealed that science and mathematics teachers reported both highly positive perceptions of AI and substantial implementation challenges.
