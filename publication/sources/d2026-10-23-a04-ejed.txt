This article develops embodied education as an orientation for examining how learners judge AI-supported activity within bodily, temporal and institutional conditions. … Its central concept is embodied judgement: the context-sensitive appraisal of technological possibilities, bodily and affective experience, educational purposes and feasible courses of action. … The framework distinguishes four interdependent components with different analytical roles: bodily awareness supplies experiential cues; situated judgement interprets those cues alongside evidence and task demands; movement-based learning provides one pedagogical means of developing and revising understanding; and ethical agency concerns justified action and responsibility towards others. … It develops classroom activities, provisional indicators and an illustrative policy comparison of Norway, Finland and Singapore. … The contribution is a relational account of the AI–health literacy interface, not a validated scale or intervention. … The principal application is secondary education, where increasingly independent technology use coexists with substantial institutional control over time and assessment.
