This study examined how students’ motivational regulation during creative writing was organized when generative artificial intelligence (AI) was unavailable or optionally available. … Drawing on Self-Determination Theory (SDT), motivation was conceptualized as configurations of intrinsic motivation, identified regulation, introjected regulation, and external regulation. … The sample comprised 406 students in Grades 5–8 who completed comparable creative writing tasks without AI (Time 1) and with optional AI access (Time 2). … Using a micro-longitudinal, person-centered design, latent profile analyses identified motivational configurations and examined psychological need satisfaction and academic achievement as predictors of profile membership; degree of AI use was examined at Time 2.
