Abstract excerpt (p. 1):
Participants were 122 seventh graders who engaged in an online bilingual inquiry unit on photosynthesis. The unit supports students in Taiwan in learning science inquiry and English as a foreign language, with GAI prompted and integrated to support students' scientific explanation writing in English.

Abstract excerpt (p. 1):
Data collected and analyzed included students’ prior academic proficiency through assessments, self-efficacy in learning through questionnaires, and student–GAI conversation turns through textual conversation logs.

Abstract excerpt (p. 1):
Results identified seven types of student–GAI shared epistemic agency (SEA), with at least 86% of students demonstrating such agency at least once. Correlation results indicated that the frequency of EA demonstrated by students was associated with their prior language proficiency. Cluster analysis with the retained four-profile solution further suggested that student–GAI SEA was likely to be activity-specific.
