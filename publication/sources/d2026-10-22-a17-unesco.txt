UNESCO Global Education Coalition, Digital Transformation Collaborative (DTC); reports on the Strategy Lab held at UNESCO Headquarters, Paris, 11 September 2026, during Digital Learning Week 2026
Original excerpt (paragraphs 2 and 3, and paragraph 2 under "Opportunity and risk", in document order, joined by " … "):
Held at UNESCO Headquarters in Paris on 11 September 2026, convening more than 200 participants, the Strategy Lab tested the DTC's Six Pillars Framework against a fast-moving question: what is AI changing, and is the framework still equipped to respond to these rapid shifts? … The session focused on how education systems can respond to AI’s expanding opportunities and widening gaps, recognising the importance of ethical governance and safety while highlighting the support teachers need to engage critically with the technology as their role evolves. … The concern raised most consistently was whether teachers are equipped with the skills and training to use AI ethically and responsibly while bringing that knowledge into their classroom practice and passing it on to students.
