Frontiers in Education | citation_online_date 2026-08-31 | The B-AIMT: development and initial validation of a domain-specific instrument for assessing mathematics teachers’ beliefs about artificial intelligence | https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1885959 | Abstract, publisher page https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/education/articles/10.3389/feduc.2026.1885959/full (read 2026-10-10)
This study presents the development of the Belief Scale for Artificial Intelligence in Mathematics Teaching (B-AIMT) and provides psychometric evidence for its initial validation as a domain-specific instrument assessing teachers’ beliefs about AI in mathematics instruction.
Based on data from 442 German pre-service and in-service teachers, confirmatory factor analyses provided support for a theoretically grounded multidimensional belief structure comprising nine latent belief dimensions organized within two overarching domains
