This study aims to develop and validate the Human–AI Collaboration Competency (HACC) Scale, which can be used to assess students’ perceived competency in allocating roles and managing tasks, and sharing, coordinating, and integrating with AI through ongoing interaction to achieve a common goal. … Two independent samples of high school students were used to examine the factor structures: Sample 1 (n = 195) was used for exploratory factor analysis (EFA), and Sample 2 (n = 356) was used for confirmatory factor analysis (CFA). … The final version of the HACC Scale consisted of 25 items loaded onto 5 factors.
