This study employed a mixed-methods design integrating a quasi-experimental intervention with qualitative thematic analysis. … A semester-long intervention was implemented with two cohorts of ninth-grade students: an experimental group ( n = 40) receiving inquiry-based chemistry laboratory instruction augmented by an AI-powered emotional feedback system, and a control group ( n = 38) receiving equivalent inquiry-based instruction supported solely by conventional teacher-student interaction. … The study comparatively analyzed students' emotional experiences across the two instructional conditions.
