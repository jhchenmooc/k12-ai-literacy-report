In response, this study developed and validated the AI-Literacy-Integrated Teaching Acceptance and Readiness Scale for Mathematics Teachers (AILTAR-M). … The instrument was theoretically organized around two domains: (1) Acceptance, comprising four constructs adapted from the Technology Acceptance Model (Perceived Usefulness, Perceived Ease of Use, Attitude toward Use, and Behavioral Intention); and (2) Readiness, encompassing Perceived AI Literacy as a self-reported cognitive dimension and Teaching Self-Efficacy as a motivational dimension. … Psychometric evaluation with 549 mathematics teachers in China supported a 29-item, six-factor structure.
