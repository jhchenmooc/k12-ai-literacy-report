This study reports survey findings on Vietnamese secondary teachers’ use, perceptions and professional learning needs regarding Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) in argumentative writing instruction (Years 6–9). … Based on responses from 515 teachers, the analysis examines GenAI applications across the writing cycle, perceived impacts on student learning, barriers to adoption and areas for support. … Results show widespread awareness of GenAI and clear preferences for tools such as ChatGPT, which teachers mainly use for planning and modelling tasks rather than for feedback or assessment.
