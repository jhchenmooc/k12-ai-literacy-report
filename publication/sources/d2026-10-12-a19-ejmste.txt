A quantitative cross-sectional survey design was employed, with data collected from 109 in-service teachers in the United Arab Emirates using a structured questionnaire measuring seven TPACK knowledge domains. … The results indicate that teachers perceived ChatGPT as providing positive support across all TPACK domains. … Content knowledge (CK) received the highest score, particularly for curriculum development, lesson planning, and instructional content preparation, whereas technological knowledge (TK) received the lowest score. … The study recommends professional development focusing on prompt design, critical evaluation of AI-generated content, bias awareness, privacy protection, and ethical AI use.
