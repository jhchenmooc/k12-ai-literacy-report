Drawing on frameworks and standards such as UNESCO's “AI Competency Framework for Teachers,” this study constructs a three-dimensional integrated framework of “AI foundational competency–AI application competency–AI ethical norms” by analyzing the primary work scenarios of K-12 teachers and typical applications of AI technologies. … Within this framework, Application Competency is anchored in three core scenarios: Teaching Practice, Educational Research, and Classroom Management. … Through four stages—literature review, expert interviews, expert validation, and pilot testing—exploratory and confirmatory factor analyses based on 18,295 valid responses demonstrate that the final 22-item scale exhibits strong reliability and validity.
