Informatics in Education (Vilnius University) | 2026-09-30 | Understanding AI Mechanisms Supports Disciplinary Reasoning and Ethical Judgment in K–12 AI Literacy Education | https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2601.025 | Abstract, publisher page https://infedu.vu.lt/journal/INFEDU/article/868/info (read 2026-10-10)
this study investigates how students use AI concepts to mediate collaborative reflection and ethical reasoning after participation in a three-year co-design based cumulative AI education program.
we qualitatively analyzed group interviews with 40 K–12 students in grades 6 and 9. The results show that students fluently used AI concepts as mediating tools.
students relied on scientific explanations and used AI concepts to explain classifier behavior, social media mechanisms, user engagement, and filter bubbles. These concepts and theoretical orientation to everyday data-driven systems extended to ethical reasoning and critical analysis of practices, structures, and problems in everyday life both at personal and societal levels.
