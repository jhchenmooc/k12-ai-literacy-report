This study was conducted in a Swedish school that had started the pilot project “AI on the schedule” for compulsory school students, for whom the national curricula do not include AI training. … A survey covering the aspects of source evaluation, data privacy, algorithmic manipulation, transparency, responsibility, and governance was distributed to these year 7 students (n = 47). … The data was analysed through cross-tabulations of question pairs. … Results show that students demonstrated strong epistemic agency in certain areas but weak agency in others, for example, in data privacy and algorithmic manipulation, even when these had been part of earlier instruction.
