Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence (Elsevier) | Available online 2026-08-24 | Measuring Acceptance of Age-Tiered AI Literacy Guidebooks: A Developmentally Informed Study of K-12 Students and Teachers | https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2026.100661 | Abstract and journal pre-proof Sec. 3.1–3.2, supplied by the manager from the publisher page (2026-10-10)
This study examined acceptance of the AI Learning Application Guidebook, developed in Elementary and Advanced editions for learners aged 9–12 and 13–18, respectively.
The guidebooks were evaluated in Taiwanese classrooms by 831 participants (794 students and 37 teachers).
The study did not measure AI literacy achievement, learning gains, actual adoption, or sustained use; therefore, the findings indicate short-term acceptance and perceived readiness for further use after initial guided exposure, not implementation effectiveness or learning outcomes.
Development was aligned with the Ministry of Education’s AI Education Action Plan and the Digital Learning Enhancement Program, with priorities on ethical safety, age-related suitability, and equitable access.
Data collection occurred in November 2025 during the inaugural large-scale deployment of the AI Learning Application Guidebooks on Taiwan’s Adaptive Learning Platform.
