We conceptualize epistemic proactivity through self-regulated learning (SRL) moves, complemented by help-seeking strategies and manifestations of epistemic vigilance. … We analyzed these behaviors in 880 substantive turns from 112 secondary-school students completing open-ended mathematical-modeling practice with a general-purpose LLM (Mistral). … Overall, students showed limited observable epistemic proactivity: their interactions were strongly request-dominated, with planning, monitoring, and evaluation relatively rare, although low-quality AI responses elicited more explicit evaluation.
