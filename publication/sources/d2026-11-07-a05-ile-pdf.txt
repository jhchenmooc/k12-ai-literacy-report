Drawing on Activity Theory, this study examines how K–12 teachers in China understand AI ethics alignment and how the mediating elements of the activity system shape these understandings. … Based on metaphor elicitation tasks completed by 26 teachers and semi-structured interviews with 16 of them, the study reveals mixed and still-developing perspectives on AI ethics. … Their AI ethics alignment was shaped through the interaction of rules, tools, community, and division of labor.
