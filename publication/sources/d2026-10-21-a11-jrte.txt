This conceptual paper develops Critical Fairness Literacy (CFL) through a structured synthesis of literature on teacher AI competency, critical AI literacy, algorithmic fairness, educational AI ethics, and educational equity. … CFL is proposed as a subdimension of teacher AI competency focused on fairness, comprising bias source identification, contextual equity analysis, and interrogation of fairness definitions. … Three practical protocols, bias tracing, equity-centered contextual review, and fairness framing reflection, connect these dimensions to classroom judgment and professional learning. … The framework and protocols remain theoretically informed proposals requiring empirical investigation.
