Abstract excerpt (abstract sentences 2, 4 and 5, from the publisher article page; joined by " … "):
This design and development study developed principles for learner–AI collaboration (LAC) and an instructional model for elementary project-based learning, and examined how the principles operated in the classroom. … The principles were organized into a four-stage divergence–convergence instructional model that specifies the roles of learners, AI, and teachers. … The classroom study involved 36 sixth-grade students, with 18 in the LAC-based instructional condition and 18 in the control condition.
