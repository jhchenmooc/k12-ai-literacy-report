This study investigated the AI tools used by kindergarten teachers, their educational purposes, implementation challenges, and technology acceptance factors influencing AI integration in Malang, Indonesia. … The design used was an exploratory sequential mixed-methods approach. … Ten Guru Penggerak and five kindergarten administrators participated in interviews, observations, and document analysis to gather qualitative data. … Findings informed a questionnaire administered to 250 kindergarten teachers selected through equal-allocation stratified random sampling across five districts of Malang City. … Qualitative findings showed that teachers used AI for lesson planning, play-based learning design, storytelling, media creation, assessment, documentation, parent communication, and professional learning.
