This study investigated changes in intrinsic motivation, extrinsic motivation, and writing self-efficacy among high school EFL learners at higher- and lower-proficiency levels following chatbot-supported writing and examined whether these changes differed across proficiency levels. … Using an exploratory mixed-methods design, the study involved 47 Vietnamese high school students divided into higher- and lower-proficiency level groups. … Over the 11-week study, participants completed 8 weeks of AI-supported writing activities using a chatbot designed in accordance with principles of Self-Determination Theory, Self-Regulated Learning Theory and Self-Efficacy Theory. … Changes in intrinsic motivation, extrinsic motivation, and writing self-efficacy were examined using pre- and post-questionnaires and semi-structured interviews.
