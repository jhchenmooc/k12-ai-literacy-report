This study aims to address this gap by, first, developing and applying a pedagogical approach to foster AI literacy and positive attitudes towards AI in young children; second, evaluating this approach through measuring the change of children’s AI literacy and their attitudes towards AI. … We propose a dual-pedagogy (DP) approach combining play-based learning and storytelling. … In our study, 162 young children (aged 5–6) participated in an AI literacy education program lasting a total of eight weeks. … They were randomly assigned to two groups. … The experimental group received instructions based on the DP approach, while the active-control group received traditional direct instruction.
