This paper presents a game-based human-AI collaboration probe designed to examine how children verify AI suggestions during task-based interaction. … The system embeds AI suggestions within a biological knowledge reasoning game and compares three Agent interaction styles: partner-like, tool-like, and dual-state Agent. … A preliminary evaluation with children aged 8-10 combined interaction logs, post-task ratings, and semi-structured interviews.
