This study examines task-selective AI integration by separating task-use presence from conditional task-use frequency. … Data were obtained from 187 public basic-school headteachers in three contexts in Ghana’s Ashanti Region who reported professional AI use during the previous six months. … Responses to 11 leadership activities produced 2,057 headteacher-task observations. … Reported information and communication technology (ICT)/digital training was positively associated with both margins. … Reported AI-specific training was associated only with conditional frequency, while self-reported AI literacy did not independently differentiate either margin.
