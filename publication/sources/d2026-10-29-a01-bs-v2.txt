This cross-sectional study used survey data from 141,988 students in Grades 6, 9, and 11 in Changchun, Northeast China. … The study addressed three research questions: whether AI literacy differed by grade and student background; which family, academic, lifestyle, and media-use factors were associated with it; and whether profiles emerged across AI understanding, use, evaluation, and ethical awareness. … AI literacy was higher among students in the upper grades, urban students, and students from higher-socioeconomic-status families, while gender differences were small. … Four latent profiles were identified: overall low-level, overall high-level, high-exposure–low-reflection, and low-exposure–high-reflection.
