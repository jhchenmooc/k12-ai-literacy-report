In this exploratory secondary analysis of survey data from 293 Minnesota PK-12 and higher education educators, we compared endorsement of improved accessibility for diverse learners as a benefit of generative AI with reported use of AI to enhance accessibility for students with disabilities. … Because the items differ in scope and target population, the comparison describes response proportions, not a failure to act on a belief. … Keyword-selected open-ended responses more often described general differentiation and language support than disability-specific use. … Most respondents were PK-12 educators (68.3%) and women (69.3%), roughly a third worked in rural settings (33.8%), and half held a master’s degree (49.1%).
