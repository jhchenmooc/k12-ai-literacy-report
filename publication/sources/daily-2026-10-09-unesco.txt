UNESCO | 9 October 2026 | UNESCO calls for pairing school screen regulation in Mexico with digital literacy and continuous monitoring
Governance measures for technological and digital platforms and devices in Mexico must be supported by comprehensive literacy, monitoring and evaluation processes, as well as by identifying the cases in which technology can be key to inclusion.
The UNESCO Representative stated that the Organization will continue to support the SEP and Mexico in this process, which, in addition to specific guidelines, must be backed by digital, algorithmic, media and information literacy (MIL) processes, so that society as a whole can better understand how technologies and platforms work and what their impacts are.
