Abstract excerpt (Introduction sentence 2 and Methods sentences 1 and 2; joined by " … "):
Drawing on research on cognitive offloading and self-regulated learning, we examined whether GenAI use for schoolwork is related to scientific literacy directly and through two components of self-regulated learning, academic help-seeking and metacognitive strategy use, and whether the purpose of use matters. … Methods: We analyzed the Program for International Student Assessment 2025 (PISA 2025) data from 12,191 15-year-old students in 374 schools in Beijing, Shanghai, Jiangsu, and Zhejiang, China. … Weighted two-level mediation models compared students within the same school, used all ten plausible values, and evaluated indirect effects with Monte Carlo confidence intervals.
