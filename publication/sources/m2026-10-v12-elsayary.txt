A quantitative, cross‐sectional survey was conducted with 380 university students recruited via the Prolific platform.
All reported outcomes are students' self‐assessments; they do not represent objectively demonstrated AI competence, employer‐rated competencies, actual employment outcomes, or temporal and causal order.
