This qualitative study explores the perceptions, practices, and orientations of 28 Lebanese schoolteachers regarding the integration of AI in school settings. … Reported barriers included insufficient training, lack of institutional support, and concerns related to data privacy and ethical use. … The analysis led to the identification of three qualitative orientations toward AI integration—reluctant users, early adopters, and critical skeptics—reflecting differentiated levels of readiness and engagement.
