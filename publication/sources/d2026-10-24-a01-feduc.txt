The aim of this study was to develop and validate the Artificial Intelligence Self-Efficacy Scale for Teachers (AISES-T). … Data were collected from teachers in different regions of Turkiye within a scale development design. … Following expert review, a pool of 50 items was reduced to a 27-item trial form. … Exploratory factor analysis (EFA) using principal axis factoring was conducted in a first sample of 313 teachers, and confirmatory factor analysis (CFA) was conducted in a second, independent sample of 559 teachers. … Participants were teachers employed in schools in different regions of Türkiye.
