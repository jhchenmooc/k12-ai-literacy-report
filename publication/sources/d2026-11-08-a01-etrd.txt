To address this gap, this study develops and validates a dedicated framework and measurement instrument for K–12 Teacher–AI Collaborative Teaching Competence (TAICT-C). … A two-phase sequential mixed-methods design was adopted. … In Phase 1, a three-round Delphi study involving 24 educational technology experts was conducted to develop and refine the TAICT-C framework. … The framework comprised four dimensions: collaborative mindset, precision design, joint teaching, and continuous development. … In Phase 2, a corresponding scale (TAICT-CS) was developed and validated using standardized procedures. … An initial pool of 65 items was generated from the framework, reviewed for content validity, and subsequently administered to 755 K–12 teachers across 23 provinces in China.
