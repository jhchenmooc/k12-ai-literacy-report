Abstract excerpt (abstract sentences 2, 3, 6 and the last sentence; joined by " … "):
This two-phase study examined calibrated AI readiness, defined as self-reported readiness to verify AI outputs, recognize ethical boundaries, and retain responsibility for AI-assisted professional decisions. … In Phase 1, 425 school-based professionals completed a survey assessing AI-related epistemic vigilance capacity, autonomous regulatory motivation, governance clarity, and calibrated AI readiness. … In Phase 2, 25 school psychologists and school counselors participated in a one-month online pilot training program delivered through Moodle and Zoom. … Because Phase 1 was cross-sectional and Phase 2 lacked a comparison group, the results do not establish causal effects.
