This study examines the psychological mechanisms underlying Chinese K–12 teachers’ discontinuance intention towards generative AI. … Drawing on the Cognition–Affect–Conation (CAC) framework, it investigates how cognitive evaluations of generative AI relate to affective responses and subsequent behavioural intention. … A quantitative cross‐sectional survey using nonprobability convenience sampling yielded data from 256 Chinese K–12 teachers with prior experience using generative AI in teaching. … The data were analysed using structural equation modelling (SEM) and fuzzy‐set qualitative comparative analysis (fsQCA).
