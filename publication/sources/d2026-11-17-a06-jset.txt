Abstract excerpt (abstract sentences 2, 3, 5 and 10, from the publisher article page; joined by " … "):
This study examined how rural secondary school students evaluate AI-generated and sample physics solutions in terms of scientific accuracy and linguistic quality. … A total of 131 students from rural schools in Kazakhstan participated in a survey in which they evaluated written responses to three conceptual physics questions and provided self-estimates of their content-related expertise. … AI-generated responses received slightly higher overall ratings than sample solutions for both scientific accuracy and linguistic quality. … These findings show that linguistic clarity and fluency may serve as important cues when students judge the scientific accuracy of AI-generated explanations.
