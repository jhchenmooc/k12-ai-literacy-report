This study proposes a Human–AI Orchestration Framework integrating Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITSs), Generative AI, and direct teacher support while retaining pedagogical orchestration and responsibility with the teacher. … Drawing on Cognitive Load Theory, the Framework proposes Cognitive Load Redistribution as a theoretical proposition and introduces coordination mechanisms, including Handover Points and a Meta-Prompt. … Its practical implementation was explored through an exploratory design pilot with 32 Grade 8 students in an authentic classroom, using observations and feedback to iteratively refine its configuration.
