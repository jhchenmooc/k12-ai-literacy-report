Abstract excerpt (abstract sentences 1, 2 and 3; transcribed from the publisher PDF; joined by " … "):
This study examines how teachers' artificial intelligence (AI)-enabled teaching capability and open educational resources (OER)-enabled pedagogical capacity are associated with sustainable education practice in mountainous areas of China. … It also assesses the indirect role of OER-enabled pedagogical capacity and the moderating role of institutional digital support. … The study describes a cross-sectional quantitative survey of 462 teachers from 54 schools in 18 mountainous counties across Yunnan, Guizhou, Sichuan, Gansu, and Qinghai, using a 24-item questionnaire and partial least squares structural equation modelling with 5,000 bootstrap resamples.
