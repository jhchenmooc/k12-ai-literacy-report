In this work, we analyze publicly available AI usage guidance documents from 35 states in the U.S. and Puerto Rico. … We find that guidance priorities converge on topics anchored in laws such as data privacy and academic integrity, each addressed in 97% of documents. … Instead, we identify notable interdependencies between states with shared citations to non-profit organizations like TeachAI and Code.org, with some states reproducing TeachAI's model guidance near-verbatim, and peer citations concentrating on a few early states such as Oregon and Washington. … No state includes parents as contributors on these documents, and only five include students.
