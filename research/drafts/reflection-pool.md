2026-10-22 記入各則的逐字摘錄（原文，選錄時須經第二輪對照與譯文確認）：

- EIAP Report Highlights（印刷頁 9，PDF 第 10 頁）："Restrictions on AI use by students: There should be a ban on the use of AI by students in primary schools, including its use at home. Alternatively, a moratorium for a minimum period of say, a year could allow education systems to take stock of risks and evidence before allowing any carefully calibrated use. AI should enter education with certification and validation from publicly approved bodies: Venture capital funded Big Tech is continuously providing 'free' AI to capture markets by addicting users, making them vulnerable and dependent on platforms that may subsequently introduce pricing or other conditions." 調查對象為亞太教師 692 份問卷；報告另寫 74.3% 教師表示工作量減輕。
- CRPE State of the American Student 2026："States are moving fast on AI, with 36 states publishing or endorsing guidance. But guidance is not the same as policy and leadership. The majority of state guidance is advisory, not mandatory, and almost none have built the capacity or funding to help districts, especially the smallest and least-resourced ones, actually meet the expectations they've set. Meanwhile, the growing movement to limit technology in schools is testing how states approach AI."
- SEP Boletín 336："Aseguró que nada de esto ocurre cuando encargamos a la inteligencia artificial que escriba por nosotros o le pedimos un resumen que nos ahorre la lectura, porque en ambos casos renunciamos a lo que somos."
- Brookings 2026-09-03："Other states have attempted to directly address how AI affects data collection. California considered amending its existing laws to prevent companies from using student data to train AI models, and Idaho clarified that its existing student data privacy laws apply to generative AI and require districts to adopt a policy that "include[s] safeguards for student privacy" and data security, among other requirements."
- Udir："Utdanningsdirektoratet har tatt i bruk sikker nettleser i utvalgte fag på videregående. Bakgrunnen er utviklingen av kunstig intelligens og stadig mer avanserte hjelpemidler. Det skaper utfordringer i oppgaveutvikling, gjennomføring og sensur av eksamen." 另段："Endringen i hjelpemiddelordningen gjøres for å redusere risikoen for bruk av KI."
